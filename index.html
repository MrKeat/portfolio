About Me

Hello! I'm Keat, a penetration tester and red teamer with a passion for breaking systems to make them more secure. My expertise lies in simulating real-world attack scenarios to identify weaknesses before malicious actors can exploit them.

From initial reconnaissance and social engineering to exploit development and post-exploitation, I cover the full spectrum of an attack lifecycle. I thrive in challenging environments, meticulously analyzing systems, networks, and applications to uncover critical vulnerabilities.

My objective is to provide actionable intelligence that helps organizations understand their true risk exposure and prioritize remediation efforts effectively. When I'm not planning my next simulated attack, I'm researching new TTPs and developing custom tooling.
